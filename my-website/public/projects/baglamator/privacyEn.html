Baglamator Privacy Policy

English Türkçe

Effective date: November 5, 2026

Publisher: Fatih Kagan Emre.

Baglamator provides instrument tuning and a metronome. You do not need to create an account.

Microphone and tuning

The tuner requests microphone permission to detect instrument pitch. Tuning audio is processed on your device; the app does not upload it or use it for cloud training. The release version does not save tuning audio or provide song recording. Microphone input stops when the tuner is no longer active or the app leaves the foreground. The metronome does not require microphone access. You can change microphone permission in iOS Settings.

Preferences

The app stores your instrument, tuning, appearance and metronome preferences locally on your device. It does not operate an account database or a server for these settings. iOS may include app preferences in your device backup. Deleting the app removes its local data; device backups are managed through Apple’s settings.

Advertising and privacy choices

The app uses Google AdMob and Google’s consent SDK. These may process IP-derived approximate location, device identifiers, advertising interactions, diagnostics and performance information. See Google’s SDK data disclosure and Google’s privacy policy.

Where required, a consent message lets you make advertising choices before ads are requested. When available, reopen those choices from Settings → Privacy → Advertising privacy choices. Whether ads appear and whether they are personalized depend on your choices, regional requirements, and the applicable ad settings. Both features are available without consenting to personalized advertising. Microphone permission is used only for tuning.

Questions and changes

Contact fatih.kagan.emre@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests. The publisher does not receive your tuning audio or maintain an account profile to delete. For Google-held data, use the controls and information in Google’s policy. We will update the date on this page when the policy changes.
